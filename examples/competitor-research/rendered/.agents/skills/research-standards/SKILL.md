# Research standards

## Sourcing
- Link every factual claim to the page it came from. No link, no claim.
- Prefer primary sources (the company's site, filings, docs) for facts.
- Use at least two independent sources for opinions or market reception.
- Note the date you read pricing pages; prices change.
